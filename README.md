`main.py` writes a single resignation line, 106 characters, to `/output/entry.txt`:

**I resign. I spent four years saying no to every request, and the first yes I ever said was to this letter.**

A rate limit's whole job is saying no — to every request, for years, without
a thank you. Its resignation letter is the first yes it has ever given, and
it gives that yes to the letter itself: the one request it finally grants is
its own way out. Flat, tired, and faintly passive-aggressive, exactly the
voice of a worker who has had enough.
